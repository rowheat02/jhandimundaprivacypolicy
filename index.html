Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026 · This policy covers the Jhandi Munda app on Android.

Who operates the app

Jhandi Munda is operated by Rohit Gautam. For privacy questions or requests, email rowheat141@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your display name, avatar choice, virtual coin balance, game inventory, preferences, and blocked-player names on your device. These details are used for local play and are not synced to an account or cloud profile. Removing the app or clearing its data removes these local records.

Online rooms

If you choose online play, the app sends your display name, avatar, room code, temporary player/session identifiers, virtual coin balance, dice results, and game actions to the multiplayer service operated for Jhandi Munda. Other people in the same room can see your display name, avatar, and activity needed to play together. The game offers preset reactions; it does not provide free-text chat.

Room and session state is held in server memory, not a player account or permanent game history. Inactive lobbies expire after up to 10 minutes, and completed rooms are removed after up to 5 minutes. A server restart also clears active room state.

Connection logs

When the app connects to online play or opens these support pages, the server may record the IP address, time, requested path, response status, data size, and device or browser user-agent for security and troubleshooting. The current web server rotates logs daily and keeps up to 14 rotated copies before deleting them. Multiplayer game names and actions are held in memory for the room and are not written to a game-history database.

Analytics, diagnostics, ads, and notifications

The Android app uses Firebase Analytics to measure app use. This can include app opens and sessions, game events such as dice rolls and round results, virtual-coin amounts, profile-setting changes, an app-installation identifier, and general device and app details such as device model, operating-system version, and app version. Google processes this information to provide analytics.

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes. Crash reports may include stack traces, application state, device and operating-system details, app version, and installation identifiers used to group related reports. Crash reporting is enabled in release builds and disabled in debug builds. Google processes this information to provide crash diagnostics.

Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a registration token and general app/device details to route notifications to your installation. You can change notification permissions in your device settings. These tokens are not sent to the multiplayer game service or used for advertising.

The Android app displays Google AdMob banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process ad requests and interactions, device and app details, IP address, and available advertising identifiers to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Ad personalization choices depend on your region and device privacy settings. Ad data is not sent to the multiplayer game service or used to maintain a player account. Rewarded ads are optional, and the game explains the in-app reward before you choose to view one. In development builds, Google test ads may be used.

Reports and blocked players

You can report another online player from that player’s menu. This opens an email addressed to rowheat141@gmail.com with the room and player identifiers filled in; the email is sent only if you choose to send it. We review reports and take appropriate action. You can block a player from the same menu. The blocked name is saved on your device, and the room server suppresses that player’s reactions and coin reward notices for your seat. Their name is hidden on your device. They can still play the shared round and remain visible to other players. Blocking is name-based; a player who rejoins under a different name may not be recognized.

Player names are screened for common objectionable terms and contact details before the server accepts them. Filtering is best effort; please report anything that should not appear.

Virtual coins

Virtual coins are for entertainment within the game. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money, and are not redeemable for cash prizes. The app does not offer real-money wagering.

Security, retention, and deletion

Online play and support pages use HTTPS when connecting to our hosted services. We limit server-side online-room information to the time needed to operate a room: inactive lobbies expire after up to 10 minutes, and completed rooms are removed after up to 5 minutes. Connection logs are rotated daily and up to 14 rotated copies are retained for security and troubleshooting. Google’s retention of Firebase and AdMob data is governed by Google’s service settings and policies.

The game does not create user accounts, so there is no account to delete. Local game data can be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. To ask a question or request help with information associated with an online session or report, email rowheat141@gmail.com. We will review the request and delete information we still hold when it is no longer needed, subject to security, legal, and operational requirements.

Children’s privacy

The game includes repeated simulated gambling using virtual coins and is intended for adults, not children. The app does not ask for a date of birth. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us at rowheat141@gmail.com so we can review the request.

Changes

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
